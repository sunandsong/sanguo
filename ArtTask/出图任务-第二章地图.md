### 1. 青州郊外 —— 参考图 `layout_qingzhou_field.png`

接在片段 A 后面：
```
open countryside south of a walled city, early autumn, slightly cooler and greener than the reference town. Follow the reference layout image EXACTLY, every color block stays in place and size:
- brown border = rocky cliffs and low mountains enclosing the area; the two brown blocks inside are rocky outcrops
- grey cell at the top center = the city gate opening in the cliffs: a packed earth ramp leading up to a dark city wall base, the wall itself only hinted at the very top edge
- yellow-green = wild grassland, mostly open, only small walkable decorations (flowers, pebbles, grass tufts); NO trees, NO bushes (they are placed by the program)
- dark green cells = bare patches of darker grass where trees will be placed by the program, keep them empty
- yellow blocks (two, left and right of the road near the top) = trampled bare earth where a rebel camp stands: flattened yellow-brown dirt, ash marks, a few scattered straw bundles; NO tents, NO fences
- tan strip = one straight packed dirt road running from the bottom edge to the gate at the top, with a branch going right to the right edge
- blue = a small pond with reeds at the edge, lighter blue = shallow muddy water
no people, no animals, no buildings, no text, fill the whole canvas edge to edge
```
